Task Information

What to do

  1. Play the prototype at least two or three times.
  2. Identify the repeating core gameplay loop.
  3. Notice how the mechanics affect player choices and feelings.
  4. Propose changes to better suit a chosen stakeholder.

Controls

  • Use the mouse/trackpad to control the spaceship.
  • Avoid hazards.
  • Try full screen for a better view.

As you play, think about…

Core Loop
What actions does the player repeat again and again?
Mechanics
What rules, objects, movement, scoring or obstacles shape the game?
Dynamics
How do those mechanics affect player behaviour while playing?
Experience
Does the game feel relaxing, tense, chaotic, strategic, satisfying, frustrating, or something else?

Reflection and Redesign Prompts

1. Describe the core gameplay loop

Example structure: start → move → avoid → collect → score → repeat.

2. Identify mechanics that could be tuned

Consider speed, difficulty, scoring, movement, hazards, power-ups, lives, timers, or level layout.

3. Choose a stakeholder

How would this game need to change for a busy parent, a competitive teen player, or a retired puzzle enthusiast?

4. Suggest redesign ideas

Propose changes and explain how they would improve the gameplay experience for that stakeholder.
